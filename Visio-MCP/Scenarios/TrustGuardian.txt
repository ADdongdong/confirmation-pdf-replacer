BUSINESS PROBLEM
Strategic customer trust erosion and executive escalations are disproportionately costly and typically detected too late—after customer frustration is visible, escalations are underway, or renewal posture is impacted. Current detection is fragmented across case systems, ad hoc updates, and manual review processes, resulting in:
•	Late awareness: Account-aligned stakeholders often learn of brewing issues only after customer-visible degradation.
•	High-cost escalations: Exec escalations trigger leadership fire drills and divert senior engineering capacity.
•	Manual inefficiency: A small Ops Center team performs inconsistent manual scans of a limited subset of cases, leaving most risk undetected.
•	Lack of explainability: When risk is surfaced, it often lacks defensible evidence and “what changed” context suitable for briefing.
End Users (Internal)
•	Trust Guardian: Incident Managers, CSAMs, AEs, account-aligned leadership stakeholders (strategic accounts)
•	nERD: A&I Ops Center reviewers, aligned managers/leaders for impacted accounts
Impact if Not Delivered
•	Continued “surprise escalation” dynamics with costly leadership time.
•	Ongoing manual review workload that cannot scale to total case volume.
•	Reduced customer confidence, increased DSAT risk, and increased renewal/relationship drag for strategic accounts.

This portfolio provides an internal early-warning system that detects engagement risk at the case level (nERD) and rolls it up into account trust posture (Trust Guardian). The solution replaces fragmented, lagging, and manual detection with continuously updated, evidence-backed insights designed for internal readiness and proactive intervention.
What is being solved
•	nERD: Detect “off-track” engagements early (assignment delays, SLA proximity, comm cadence patterns) and surface actionable, explainable risk to Ops Center and relevant stakeholders.
•	Trust Guardian: Provide account-ready trust intelligence across engagements for a TPID, showing posture, themes, evidence, and briefing-ready narratives without contaminating case systems.

Expected outcomes (initial targets refined during pilot):
•	Reduced executive escalations and late severity increases for in-scope A&I engagements by prioritizing earlier detection and targeted intervention.
•	Operational efficiency gains by reducing manual review time and focusing reviewer attention on the highest-risk items.
•	Improved account readiness and reduced relationship drag for strategic accounts through earlier narrative shaping and internal coordination.

In Scope
•	Derived event-driven monitoring of in-scope engagements for:
o	Case creation
o	Inbound/outbound email interaction metadata events
o	Assignment status changes (when available)
o	SLA state changes (when available)
o	Severity changes (when available)
o	Case lifecycle state changes (when available)
•	nERD: Case-level risk assessment, evidence generation, triage workflow, internal notifications.
•	Trust Guardian: TPID-level posture, themes, evidence, trends, briefing summaries, and dashboard-first UX.
•	Security model: TPID-scoped access via one Dataverse Team per TPID, with an Ops Center role exception.
•	Feedback loop: Human dispositions/feedback captured for tuning and governance.
Out of Scope
•	Customer-facing communications or actions of any kind.
•	Automatic case severity changes, case routing changes, or customer-visible updates.
•	Writing Trust Guardian narratives back into the case record by default.
•	“Escalation marker” events from MSaaS (not available); escalation risk is inferred by portfolio logic and surfaced internally as risk outcomes.

5.4 Processing Logic (Happy Path)
OP-1 — Derived Event Enqueue (Router)
•	Inputs: CX_DerivedEvent (New)
•	Actions:
o	Determine routing from DerivedEventType → list of WorkItems (lane/worktype/priority)
o	Create CX_WorkItem records with alternate key idempotency: WorkType|DerivedDedupKey
o	Mark CX_DerivedEvent.Enqueued
•	Outputs: Work queue populated deterministically; derived events remain immutable audit records.
OP-2 — Shared Worker (Engagement Index + Enrichment)
•	Inputs: WorkItems such as Shared.UpsertEngagement, Shared.ResolveTpidIfMissing
•	Actions:
o	Upsert CX_Engagement keyed by CaseNumber
o	Populate/refresh TPID, severity, lifecycle state, assignment state, comm timestamps (as available)
o	Apply owner assignment to TPID Team when TPID is known
•	Outputs: Single source of truth for case/engagement state used by both nERD and Trust Guardian.
OP-3 — nERD Worker (Near-Real-Time Case Risk)
•	Inputs: WorkItems such as nERD.ProcessEmailSignal, nERD.EvaluateCaseRisk
•	Actions:
o	Update case-level rolling indicators (e.g., inbound/outbound comm markers, assignment pending duration, SLA risk flags where available)
o	Produce risk state (NotAtRisk / AtRisk / Critical) and drivers
o	Write evidence items (timestamped, short, sanitized)
o	If risk transitions to Critical (or high-confidence AtRisk per config), send internal notifications to subscribers and Ops Center
•	Outputs: nERD_CaseRiskCurrent, nERD_CaseRiskHistory, nERD_EvidenceItem, updated triage queue.
OP-4 — Trust Guardian Snapshot Builder (Cadence)
•	Inputs: TG_AccountScope(InScope=true), CX_Engagement, nERD rollups
•	Actions:
o	Compute TPID posture (Green/Amber/Red) using explainable thresholds and trend deltas
o	Derive themes (responsiveness gaps, stalled progress patterns, repeated loops, high-visibility friction) grounded in evidence
o	Materialize dashboard read model: posture current/history, themes, evidence, briefing summary
o	Optional: posture transition alerting (Amber→Red), subscription-gated
•	Outputs: Dashboard-first, briefing-ready trust intelligence for account-aligned roles.

5.6 Error Handling, Retries & Fallbacks
Recoverable Errors
•	Retry policy: exponential backoff; max attempts configurable per work type.
•	Idempotency: alternate keys prevent duplicate derived events and duplicate work items; worker operations are upsert-based.
Non-recoverable Errors
•	Missing required fields for derived type, invalid dedup key, schema version mismatch.
•	Work item is marked Failed with a clear error code; surfaced to ops dashboards.
Fallback Behavior
•	If enrichment fails (e.g., cannot resolve TPID for email interaction), store event as “pending enrichment” and retry; do not discard.
•	If notification sending fails, persist assessment and queue notification retry separately (notification delivery is not allowed to block risk state persistence).

12. Security, Privacy & Compliance [Include links to Security documentation below]
12.1 Authentication & Authorization
•	Identity: user context for Copilot actions where feasible.
•	Access model:
o	TPID-scoped Team ownership for account-aligned tables (Trust Guardian + engagement)
o	Org-level access for Ops Center role to nERD triage tables only
•	Pipeline tables (CX_DerivedEvent, CX_WorkItem) restricted to service principals / operators.
12.2	Data Protection & PII

•	Derived events contain sanitized context only in msdynsvc_data.
•	No raw customer email body is stored or exposed by default in nERD/TG evidence.
•	Contact identifiers (if present) handled under strict need-to-know; prefer storing references/IDs over personal details.
•	Retention:
o	Raw event retention per compliance requirements (restricted).
o	Derived events and work items retained for operational window + audit requirements.
o	Evidence/history retained as required for tuning and reporting.

12.3 Compliance Requirements
•	Internal-only surfaces.
•	RBAC + row-level security enforced through Dataverse.
•	Data residency and sovereign cloud constraints addressed via environment-specific deployment policies (as applicable).

•	TPID flips in-scope → seed active cases → TG posture available without raw replay
•	Duplicate derived events → no duplicate work items; idempotent updates
13.3 Safety / Red Team Testing
•	Attempt to access raw event payload via agent actions (must fail).
•	Attempt cross-TPID leakage (must be trimmed by team membership).
•	Prompt injection attempts to force hallucinated evidence (agent must cite evidence items and refuse to invent).
